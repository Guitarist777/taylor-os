TAYLOR colour scheme, Plasma theme and window decoration (recoloured Slot ones, see their CHANGES.txt) in place of
Slot's; the TAYLOR sky wallpaper in place of Slot's; Breeze's splash in place of Magna-Splash-6 (not shipped);
and a bottom-panel layout written out in full (Slot's layout folder is spelt "layots", so Plasma never read it).
